# Limitations and safe interpretation

- **Single source:** the system was grounded in one Malaysian clinical practice guideline on spontaneous ICH (2025). It does not represent all current evidence or local protocols.
- **Small question set:** answer evaluation used 29 questions, so results may not generalise to broader clinical queries.
- **One evaluator:** answers were scored by one medically qualified evaluator. Inter-rater reliability was not measured.
- **Retrieval failure remains possible:** relevant passages may be missed, ranked poorly, or lack enough context.
- **Generation failure remains possible:** the model may misinterpret evidence, omit important qualifications, or generate unsupported statements despite the prompt.
- **Model and provider dependence:** hosted model availability and behaviour can change.
- **Evaluation is not clinical validation:** aggregate scores do not demonstrate safety, clinical utility, or improved patient outcomes.
- **No prospective clinical testing:** this prototype has not been validated for clinical use.

Do not use this system for diagnosis, triage, treatment, or patient care. For clinical decisions, consult the original current guideline and qualified clinicians.
